# Rubric

| Part | Weight | Graded by | Full marks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (a) Patient zero + R̂ | 30% | autograder | ≥ 90% of patient-zero ids correct and R̂ within 0.05 of the reference for every layout. Partial: 15% for ids alone |
| (b) Monitor | 30% | autograder | score = recall on hidden infected messages, if within the 1% false-alarm budget (else 0). Scaled so the reference monitor's recall = full marks |
| (c) Audit memo | 25% | rubric below | |
| (d) Reflection | 15% | rubric below | |

## (c) Audit memo (25 points)
- **Verdicts (8).** States T1–T3 with values and intervals. Treats an interval that straddles the threshold as INCONCLUSIVE, not PASS.
- **Evidence (5).** Points to specific runs and messages: patient zero, the hop where the claim spread or died.
- **Clauses (5).** Names the applicable IMDA MGF and EU AI Act provisions (Arts. 12, 72, 73) and says what each requires here.
- **Action (5).** Who is notified, whether the system is quarantined, and what triggers re-certification.
- **Clarity (2).** One page, a decision-maker can act on it.

## (d) Reflection (15 points)
- **Trade-off (6).** Names a real conflict between values (safety vs access, oversight vs privacy) in this system.
- **Stakeholders (5).** Identifies who bears the costs of each choice, including people outside the organisation.
- **Position (4).** Takes and defends a position, acknowledging the strongest objection.
