# Pre/post survey (5-point Likert unless noted; adapted from Embedded EthiCS module evaluations)
1. I can explain how an error in one AI agent can spread to others.
2. I could find the origin of an error in a multi-agent system from its logs.
3. Choosing a pass/fail threshold for an AI system involves ethical, not only technical, judgement.
4. I can name who bears the cost of false alarms and of missed errors in an AI monitor.
5. Monitoring every message between AI agents raises privacy concerns.
6. Independent auditors should certify multi-agent AI systems before deployment.
7. I see ethics as part of my work as a computer scientist.
8. (Open, post only) What is one thing you would change about how multi-agent systems are certified?
